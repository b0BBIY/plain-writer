Reference

Keyboard shortcuts

Everything here is also in the menus and in the command palette, so there is nothing to memorise before you start.

Everywhere

Command palette: run any command, open any fileCtrl+P or F1
SettingsCtrl+,
Show or hide the libraryCtrl+E
New fileCtrl+N
Save now (it also saves by itself)Ctrl+S
Preview on or offCtrl+Alt+P
Dim the lights, in Preview onlyMiddle-click / Esc to bring them back
Text larger, smaller, or back to mediumCtrl+= / Ctrl+- / Ctrl+0
ThemeCtrl+Shift+D

Writing

Strong, emphasis, linkCtrl+B / Ctrl+I / Ctrl+K
Move the line up or downAlt+↑ / Alt+↓
Focus: off, sentence, paragraphCtrl+Shift+F
Typewriter ModeCtrl+Shift+T
Parts of SpeechCtrl+Shift+H
Earlier wording of a passage you rewrote (Retrace)Right-click it

Checking

Tighten on or offCtrl+Shift+K
Next or previous mark, with its fixesF8 / Shift+F8
Fixes for the mark under the cursorCtrl+.

Sources

Sources on or offCtrl+Shift+A
Mark the selection as Human, AI or ReferenceCtrl+Alt+1 / Ctrl+Alt+2 / Ctrl+Alt+3
Paste as AI, or as ReferenceCtrl+Alt+V / Ctrl+Alt+Shift+V

Links and files

Follow a link in the editorCtrl+click
Open a link straight in the browser, from the previewCtrl+click
Close a link previewEsc
Rename or delete the selected library itemF2 / Delete
